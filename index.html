About TTM

At Téllez Tires Mobile, we proudly serve the Gilroy community with dependable tire sales, installation, and repair services. Whether you drive a car, pickup truck, or work vehicle, our goal is to help keep you safely on the road.

We want every customer to leave satisfied and feel comfortable returning whenever they need tire service again.
